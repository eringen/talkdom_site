persist

Attribute · 0.5.0

Save receiver markup after successful applies and restore it on startup.

Example

<div receiver="preferences" persist></div>
  • Keys use talkDOM:<primary name> and are shared across pages on the same origin.
  • Outer swaps save replacement markup under the original key.
  • Storage errors warn without failing successful DOM updates; corrupt entries are ignored independently.
  • Markup is stored in plain text and restored as trusted content. Do not persist secrets.
