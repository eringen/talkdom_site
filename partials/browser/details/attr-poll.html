poll:

Receiver keyword · 0.5.0

Run the preceding method at a positive interval in milliseconds or seconds.

Example

<div receiver="feed get: /updates apply: inner poll: 10s"></div>

talkDOM.maxPollers = 128;
  • Must be the last keyword. Supported intervals end in ms or s.
  • Dynamic insertion, replacement, removal, and edited declarations are reconciled automatically.
  • Identical declarations share one poller and deliver to the live receiver group. Different commands or intervals remain separate.
  • Pending ticks do not overlap within a poller; success/error events use shared delivery.
